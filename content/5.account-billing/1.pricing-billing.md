# Pricing & Hourly Billing

1st Services operates on a transparent **pay-as-you-go** billing model calculated down to the second, with monthly price caps so you never overpay.

---

| Resource Type | Specs | Hourly Rate | Monthly Cap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VPS Micro** | 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 25 GB NVMe | `$0.0055 / hr` | `$4.00 / mo` |
| **VPS Standard** | 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 80 GB NVMe | `$0.0208 / hr` | `$15.00 / mo` |
| **VPS Pro** | 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 160 GB NVMe | `$0.0416 / hr` | `$30.00 / mo` |
| **NVMe Block Volume** | Scalable block storage | `$0.00014 / GB / hr` | `$0.10 / GB / mo` |

## Invoices & Payment Methods

- **Supported Payment Methods**: Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Crypto (USDC, USDT, Bitcoin), and Wire Transfer for Enterprise accounts.
- **Billing Cycle**: Billed on the 1st of every calendar month for the preceding month's usage.
