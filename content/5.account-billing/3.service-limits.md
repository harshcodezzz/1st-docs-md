# Resource Quotas & Service Limits

To safeguard platform infrastructure and protect accounts from accidental overspending, default resource quotas are assigned to new accounts.

---

## Default Account Limits

| Resource | Default Free Tier | Default Pro Tier | Enterprise Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Max Concurrent VPS Instances** | 2 instances | 25 instances | Unlimited (Custom) |
| **Total vCPUs** | 4 vCPUs | 64 vCPUs | Unlimited (Custom) |
| **Total RAM** | 8 GB | 128 GB | Unlimited (Custom) |
| **Managed Databases** | 1 instance | 10 instances | Unlimited (Custom) |
| **Block Storage Volumes** | 100 GB | 5,000 GB | 100+ TB |
| **Floating Anycast IPs** | 1 IP | 10 IPs | Unlimited |
| **Active Short Links** | 1,000 | 50,000 | Unlimited |
| **Active Temp Mail Inboxes** | 10 concurrent | 500 concurrent | 10,000+ concurrent |

---

## Requesting a Quota Increase

Need more compute capacity or higher API throughput?

1. Open the [1st Services Console](https://1st-services.com).
2. Go to **Settings** → **Resource Limits**.
3. Click **Request Limit Increase** and specify the desired quota and workload use-case.
4. Our engineering team reviews and approves requests in under 2 business hours.
